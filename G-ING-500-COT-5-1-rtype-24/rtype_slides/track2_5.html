Sharing the World
Everyone sees a slightly past version of the world. How do you keep them in sync?
Snapshots room → clients: the world state Every tick, the room sends where everything is. Clients just draw it.
simple, self-healing: a lost one doesn't matter heavy → send deltas vs the last ACKed one
Quake 3, most shooters
Events room → clients: what changed Only spawn, move, fire, destroy. What the subject lists.
tiny packets lose one → desync forever. Needs reliability.
MMOs, turn-based games
Lockstep everyone ↔ everyone: inputs only Each machine simulates the same game from the same inputs.
almost no bandwidth waits for the slowest, needs a deterministic engine
RTS: Age of Empires, StarCraft
Rollback lockstep + guessing Guess others' inputs, then rewind & replay when the real ones arrive.
no waiting, feels local save/restore the whole state, fast
fighting games (GGPO)
Typical R-Type mix inputs ↑ client → room snapshots ↓ room → clients reliable events for what must arrive (death, game over)
