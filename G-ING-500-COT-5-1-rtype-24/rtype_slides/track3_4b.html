Kinds of Levels
Who builds the level: a designer, an algorithm, or both?
Hand-made designer places everything
+ full control of pacing, bosses, surprises
− costly to make, same every run
e.g. the original R-Type
Procedural seed → generator → level
+ endless variety, tiny to store
− can feel bland or unfair, hard to tune
e.g. roguelikes · No Man's Sky
Blueprints hand-made chunks, stitched by rules
+ handcrafted quality and variety
− chunks must fit together
e.g. Spelunky · Dead Cells · Helldivers 2 missions
Galactic map levels are places in a world
+ choice, progress, a reason to come back
− needs a persistent server state
e.g. Helldivers 2 · FTL — next slides
Procedural + online: send the seed, not the level. Same seed + same generator = same level on every machine. Only if the random is yours: std::mt19937 is identical everywhere, std::uniform_int_distribution is not.
