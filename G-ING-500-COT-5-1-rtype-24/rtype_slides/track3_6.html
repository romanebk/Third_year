Epitech Afrique — Track #3 · UX
Galactic War
Pick a planet. Everyone moves the front.
The map is the mission select. The squad picks a sector and a planet, not a room in a list.
One map for every player. Missions won push the liberation. The whole server fights the same war.
The jump is a loading screen. While the ship is in FTL, the room is spawned and the handoff happens.
In the sector, you see who's there. A squad joins: it warps in. It leaves: it warps out.
Lobby-side, not room-side. The map is slow data: planet ids and percentages, stored in the DB. "Squad X joined Kepler-9" is a presence event: the client plays the warp.
