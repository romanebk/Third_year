Epitech Afrique — Engine
engine/
Headers say what. Sources say how.
.hpp declarations, templates .cpp implementations
Templates (SparseArray, EventBus) live in headers — they have to.
Components: header-only structs. No .cpp — there's no logic to put in one.
The core only knows interfaces: ISystem, IGraphics, INetwork. Plugins implement them.
Proof of decoupling: engine/ builds alone — no #include of anything R-Type. If it doesn't, something leaked.
