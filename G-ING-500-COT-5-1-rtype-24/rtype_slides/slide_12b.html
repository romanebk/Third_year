A Better Entity
Still an id for the systems. A card for everything else.
uuid: the same on every run and every machine. The index is for memory, the uuid is for files and the network.
name: for you. Logs, the debugger, the editor's hierarchy.
tags: cheap labels. "All enemies", "everything flying": a system, a script or a mod asks by tag.
parent: a tree. The child follows its parent; destroy the parent, the children go too.
Click the tags: this is how a system finds its entities without knowing them.
