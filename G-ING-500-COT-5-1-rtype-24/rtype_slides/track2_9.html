Packets & Serialization
Your protocol, your design.
You've done this in Tek1. Robot Factory wrote a .cor: magic number, header, big-endian, opcode + coding byte. Corewar read it back and checked it. A packet is the same thing — through a socket, not a file.
Binary — the subject says MUST. Fixed-width types: uint16_t, not int.
Pick one byte order for the wire and stick to it.
Never send raw structs or pointers: padding, alignment, CPU.
Validate every length. Malformed packet → drop it, never crash.
Keep a version field. Your protocol will change.
Design your own — or study the standards first: RFC-style specs, the TLV pattern, MessagePack and Protobuf wire formats, how QUIC or ENet build headers. Then document yours: the subject requires it.
