Arsenal
Beyond "shoot forward": every weapon is a trade-off. Think Helldivers 2.
Shield ENERGY Bubble eats hits. Each hit drains energy. Empty = gone.
Poison STATUS Damage over time, spreads to neighbours. A Poisoned component.
Hellbomb YOUR LIFE Suicide attack: ram it, huge blast, you die too. Then reinforce.
Quasar ENERGY Charge, then a killing beam. No ammo: it eats your energy. Spam it → you black out and drift.
Drones AI Helpers that orbit you: one shoots, one repairs. Their own little system.
Missiles AMMO Homing, strong, but limited. Empty → find a resupply drop.

Pay with something: energy, ammo, or your life. That's the trade-off.
Effects are components added on hit: Poisoned, Slowed, Burning. Systems tick them.
Call-ins like HD2's stratagems: a key combo drops a weapon, a drone, a resupply.
