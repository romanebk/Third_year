Entity
A thing in the game. Just a number.
"Player", "Bydos"… are not stored in the entity — they come from its components.
The registry creates and destroys them. Killing an entity = removing its components.
Keep IDs, never pointers to components. And freed IDs get reused — careful with anyone still holding the old one.
Over the network, the ID is what server and clients agree on: entity 6 moved to (320, 90). The room hands out the IDs.
