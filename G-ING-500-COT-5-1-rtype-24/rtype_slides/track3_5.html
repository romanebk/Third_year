Epitech Afrique — Track #3 · UX
Game Feel
Nothing changes the rules. Everything changes the fun.
SFX & music: every action makes a sound. Music follows the intensity.
Spatial audio: an explosion on the left plays on the left, quieter when far.
VFX: particles, hit flash, muzzle flash. The hit must feel like a hit.
Cam shake: short, decays fast, bigger for bigger events.
HUD: score, lives, beam charge, teammates. Readable at a glance.
Sync on the sound. Measure its loudness curve and its cracks, then drive the shake, the snap, the spring from them (see the warp on the Galactic War slide).
All client-side. The room says "enemy 12 died at (x, y)". Each client plays its own boom. Never send particles.
Accessibility is required. Options to turn off shake and flashes, volume per channel, colour-blind-safe HUD.
