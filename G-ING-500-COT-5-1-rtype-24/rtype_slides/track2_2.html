Spawn, don't fork
A room is its own binary. The lobby just starts it.
fork()copies the lobby
Lmemorysocketsthreads
→
L copymemory (CoW)sockets leaked1 thread, locks stuck
  • The child inherits every fd of the lobby.
  • Multi-threaded lobby: only the caller survives — locks stay locked.
  • No Windows equivalent.
spawnstarts a fresh program
// Linux · Windows: CreateProcess
posix_spawn(&pid, "./r-type_room", …, argv, env);
// argv: --port 4243 --name "Force Field"
// --mode endless --lobby 127.0.0.1:4242
  • Clean process: its own memory, nothing inherited.
  • Everything it needs comes from argv.
  • Cross-platform: Boost.Process · reproc.
