Ship Systems
Energy is the real health bar.
The reactor makes energy. Every system spends it: engines by throttle, shield per hit, laser per shot, repair to heal.
Missiles use ammo, not energy. That's why you carry both.
Energy at 0 → blackout: everything shuts down, the ship drifts, the reactor reboots.
The Quasar kills, but costs 35 energy a shot. Spam it and you black out yourself. Then the reactor regenerates.
Subsystems have their own HP: hit the engines, lose thrust.
All numbers live in components (Energy, Hull, Engines) simulated by the room. Balancing = editing a file.
