Rules & Modes
Same game, different rules, chosen per room.
The room loads its rules at creation: a small file, like everything else.
Each rule is checked by a system: friendly fire lives in the collision system, lives in the respawn system.
Difficulty scales numbers, not code: enemy HP, bullet speed, spawn rate.
Modes change the goal: co-op reaches the boss, versus compares scores, PvP shoots each other.
A game design question: which combinations are fun? Play them. Some rules are only fun with others. Show the rules in the room list.
