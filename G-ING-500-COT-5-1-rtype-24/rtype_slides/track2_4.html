Client Handoff
From L to r2 — without a stranger sneaking in.
L answers JOIN with the room's ip:port and a session token.
The room only accepts a HELLO with a valid token. Random UDP packet? Ignored.
The room answers with your entity ID and streams the map — the client's loading screen.
After WELCOME, the client never talks to L again until the game ends.
Don't forget the way back. Game over → results to L, everyone back to the lobby. Disconnect → rejoin with the token. Quit → free the slot.
